WELCOME TO THE HALL OF

MARITIME
ARCHAEOLOGY

The sea has carried people, goods, and ideas across the world for millennia. Beneath its surface lie the traces of countless journeys: shipwrecks, lost cargoes, submerged settlements, and vessels that once connected distant shores. Explore the archaeology of a world shaped by water, and discover the stories that the sea has preserved.
