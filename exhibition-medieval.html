WELCOME TO THE HALL OF

MEDIEVAL ARCHAEOLOGY

Empires rose and fell, cities flourished, and networks of trade, faith, and knowledge connected distant societies. Beyond the tales of knights and kings lay a world of ingenuity, exchange, and everyday lives chronicles rarely recorded. From fortified ruins and great places of worship to forgotten workshops, buried treasures, and ancient graves, explore the archaeological traces that reveal a Middle Ages far richer than legend suggests.
