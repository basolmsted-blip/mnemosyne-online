WELCOME TO THE HALL OF

EGYPTOLOGY

Along the banks of the Nile, a civilization flourished for thousands of years, raising monumental temples, carving images of gods and kings, and preserving its stories in stone. From the great pyramids of the Old Kingdom to the painted chambers of the Valley of the Kings, explore the lives, beliefs, and remarkable achievements of ancient Egypt, and the archaeological discoveries that continue to illuminate its past.
