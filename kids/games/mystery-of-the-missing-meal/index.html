Case Closed?

You've solved an ancient food mystery! Or have you?

Congratulations, archaeologist! You searched an ancient settlement, investigated food evidence, and reconstructed a possible meal.

Archaeologists rarely discover the complete story. They use what survives to build the most convincing explanations possible. Sometimes there is more than one reasonable interpretation. At other times, the most important thing to say is, “We don't know yet.”
